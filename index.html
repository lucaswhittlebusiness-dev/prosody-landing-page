Fifty-seven people. None of them talk the same way.

Their tone shifts as the conversation does: warmer when it's going well, more guarded when it isn't. Pause, go off-script, push back. They adapt.

The Random scenario

A situation you haven't seen, with someone you haven't met.

Not a grade. A map of how it went.

Every session ends with a score and specific feedback on where the conversation went and how to handle it better. Run the same scenario again and it won't go the same way, because nobody on the other end is following a script.

Once a week isn't enough.

Pick a reminder time while you set up (morning, afternoon, or evening) and Prosody nudges you once a day. Streaks keep count of how many days in a row you've shown up, and every session earns XP that moves you along the map.

The next one doesn't have to be your first try.
