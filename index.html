Which conversation is on your mind?

Fifty situations across nine categories. Each one puts a specific person on the other side, and they speak first. Harder, higher-pressure ones unlock as you progress.

The screening call

“Thanks for making the time. Let's start simple — walk me through what you're doing now.”

That's the whole app. These are the real screens.

Fifty-seven people. None of them talk the same way.

Each one has their own voice and personality, and they don't just praise everything you say. They reply in short, reactive lines like a real call, and you can hold the mic to jump in and interrupt.

Swipe to meet them

Practice it your way.

Custom practice

Describe any situation, like “asking my manager for a pay rise,” and pick a practice partner to play it out.

Solo practice

Work on your delivery on your own, with no one on the other end.

Drills

Short, focused exercises for clarity and pronunciation.

Lessons

Short lessons with practice built in, from beginner to advanced.

7-day programs

Guided paths for real improvement, one day at a time.

Not a grade. A map of how it went.

After every conversation you get scores for the things that matter, what you did well, what to improve with a concrete rewrite, and one thing to try next time. Run the same scenario again and it won't go the same way, because nobody on the other end is following a script.
