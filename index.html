Real-Time Voice

It's not a script.
It's a conversation.

The AI character responds to exactly what you say. Take a pause, go off-script, say the wrong thing. It adapts. Which means you have to, too.

Every conversation is different. You can't memorize your way through it.

57 Characters

way of talking.

Ingrid

Ellis

Has heard this pitch before. Probably seven versions of it. Gets to the hard question before you're ready.

Vera

Rosalind

For conversations that matter

Practice it
before it counts.

Before the interview

Difficult questions don't have to catch you off guard.

Practice your answers without the stakes. Figure out what you actually want to say before you're sitting across from someone who matters.

Before the first date

Get comfortable talking to someone new.

Conversation is a skill. Practice the early minutes — the small talk, the questions, the silences — until they feel easy.

Before the difficult conversation

Find the words before you need them.

The apology, the confrontation, the thing you've been putting off. Say it out loud first. Know how it sounds before it matters.

Before the presentation

Practice speaking clearly when everyone's listening.

Public speaking is practiced, not innate. Rehearse in a space where it's safe to go wrong until going wrong stops happening.

Before the sales call

Handle the objections before they happen.

Know your pitch cold. Practice the pushback, the redirect, the close. Walk in having already heard the hard questions.

Before anything, really

Conversation is a skill like any other.

The more you practice, the better you get. Prosody gives you the space to practice without the pressure of the real thing.
