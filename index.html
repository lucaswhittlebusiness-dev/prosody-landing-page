Prosody is a voice-first app for rehearsing the conversations that make you nervous.

1

Pick a situation

A job interview, a first date, a hard talk. More than a hundred to choose from, each with a character who speaks first.

2

Talk out loud

They answer in real time, like a real call. Hold the mic to jump in and interrupt.

3

See how it went

Scores for pace, clarity and confidence, plus one thing to try next time. Then go again.

Here's what it looks like.

130 people. None of them talk the same way.

Each has their own voice and personality. They won't just praise everything you say.
